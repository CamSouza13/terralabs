Land-based recirculating aquaculture is where Terra earns its first revenue. One bad night can wipe a cohort. We forecast the crash hours ahead and act before you lose the tank.

What actually goes wrong

In a recirculating aquaculture system (RAS), failure builds at the level of the whole loop before any single probe trips. A dissolved-oxygen crash, an ammonia spike, a nitrite climb, or a slow biofilter collapse can be underway while the dashboard still reads normal, because the probes you trust foul and drift. By the time a threshold alarm fires, the DO has already crossed the line and the cohort is at risk.

What Terra does

  • Reconstructs the true whole-system state from the probes you already run, separating real drift from sensor fouling.
  • Forecasts a DO crash or biofilter collapse hours ahead, with honest uncertainty, not a single-gauge trip.
  • Acts on-site within limits you set (aerate, dose, or alert), offline, with every action in a full audit log.

The pilot

A pilot starts on a single system and runs read-only alongside your current setup. We calibrate Terra to your facility, then shadow real operations and report the warning lead on what actually happened. Pricing is value-share: you pay when we prevent a loss. Typical length is eight to twelve weeks.

Data we work from: your existing water-chemistry channels (total ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, dissolved oxygen), plus temperature, pH, feed rate, flow or exchange, tank volume, and stocking or biomass. Any incident history sharpens calibration but is never required.

Proof and stage, honestly
