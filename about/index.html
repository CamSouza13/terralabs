ABOUT

One honest engine for the systems that keep life alive.

Terra Laboratories builds the autonomy layer for land-based fish farms. We forecast a DO crash or biofilter collapse hours ahead and act on it on-site, offline, behind a full audit log. The same engine is built to carry into water, bioprocess, and eventually life support in space.

How we work

We deepen a single engine instead of splitting focus, price on the value we create through value-share pilots, and are candid about our stage. Terra is validated in simulation today on a real-data foundation. Bench validation is planned for Q4 2026 and the first paid pilots for H1 2027.

What we are not

Terra is built for land-based, recirculating systems. If you run net pens or open ponds, we are not the right fit yet. We would rather tell you that now than waste your time.
