About

One honest engine for the systems that keep life alive.

Terra Laboratories builds the autonomous intelligence platform for living systems. One engine reads the true state of a closed living loop, forecasts failure hours ahead, and acts on it on-site, offline, behind a full audit log. We start in water on Earth and carry the same core to bioremediation and, eventually, life support in space.

The founder

Cameron Souza, Founder

I am a data scientist. I started Terra because the systems we count on to keep living things alive still fail from a variable nobody is watching, and the failure is usually preventable if you see it coming. In a fish tank that is a dissolved-oxygen crash; in a sealed habitat it is the air itself. I would rather show you exactly where we are, pre-revenue and validated in simulation, than oversell it.

How we work

We deepen a single engine instead of splitting focus, price on the value we create through value-share pilots, and are candid about our stage. Terra is validated in simulation today on a real-data foundation. Bench validation is planned for Q4 2026 and the first paid pilots for H1 2027.

What we are not

Terra is built for closed and recirculating systems, where the whole loop can be modeled and acted on. If your system is fully open to the environment, like net pens or open ponds, we are not the right fit yet. We would rather tell you now than waste your time.
