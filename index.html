/ 01The premise

Life is the rarest thing we know of.

Every living system, a reef, a rice paddy, a sealed habitat, holds itself together through a web of variables no one fully sees. It fails as a whole, quietly, hours before any single sensor trips. For four billion years these systems kept their own balance. We are the first generation that could keep it on purpose.
