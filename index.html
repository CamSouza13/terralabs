THE PROBLEM

The tank crashes as a whole, quietly.

In a recirculating system (RAS), a DO crash, an ammonia spike, or a slow biofilter collapse builds for hours before any single probe trips an alarm. Probes foul and drift, so the dashboard reads calm while the fish are already dying.

  • A single bad night can wipe a cohort and cost a land-based farm millions
  • Hours pass between the first hidden drift and mass mortality
  • Threshold alarms fire after the DO has already crossed the line

FAQ

The questions operators actually ask.

Does it act without me?

Only inside limits you set. Every autonomous action, like bumping aeration, is bounded by operator-defined caps and written to a full audit log. You can also run Terra in alert-only mode, where it forecasts and warns but never touches an actuator.

Does it work offline, on a bad VSAT link?

Yes. The whole engine runs on a small edge node on-site. It needs no cloud and no network to forecast or act, so a dropped or slow VSAT link does not stop it. Connectivity is for reporting, not for the decision.

Is this proven yet?

We are candid about our stage. Terra is validated in simulation today on a real-data foundation, and the company is pre-revenue. Bench validation is planned for Q4 2026 and the first paid pilots for H1 2027. If you want to be one of those first pilots, that is exactly what the pilot request is for.

Do I need a data scientist to run it?

No. Terra uses commodity probes plus a small edge node, and we handle calibration and the model. Your team sees forecasts, alerts, and an audit log, not math.

What does a pilot cost?

We price on value share: you pay when we prevent a loss. A pilot starts on a single system so the risk to you is small and the result is measurable on your own farm.
