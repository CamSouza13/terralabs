FAQ

The questions operators ask.

Does it act without me?

Only inside limits you set. Every autonomous action is bounded by operator-defined caps and written to a full audit log. You can also run it alert-only.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole engine runs on a small edge node on-site. No cloud, no network required to forecast or act.

Is this proven yet?

Validated in simulation today on a real-data foundation. Pre-revenue. Bench validation Q4 2026, first paid pilots H1 2027.

What does a pilot cost?

Value-share: you pay when we prevent a loss. A pilot starts on a single system, measurable on your own site.
