01 — The Problem

Closed life support fails at the system level.

The unsolved problem isn't any single component — it's that closed biological systems fail in ways no one has fully mapped. Atmosphere, water, plants, and microbes are so tightly coupled that failures emerge from the interactions between them, cascading across the whole loop before any single gauge crosses a threshold. Sensor biofouling and drift are one such failure mode — real and expensive — but only one. What's missing is a system that models the entire state of the loop and sees the failure coming.

NASA's explicit technology gap

"Autonomous biological life-support systems represent a critical unmet need for sustained human presence on the Moon and Mars."

The tooling today is manual checks and threshold alarms on individual sensors — blind to failures that emerge across the system. We replace it with inference — a live probabilistic estimate of the whole loop's state, mounted on edge AI, catching systemic failures before they cascade.
02 — The Solution

The Terra Engine.

Bayesian state estimation that fuses drifting, noisy sensor signals into a reliable picture of biogeochemical state — running edge-autonomous with no network required. It holds a probability distribution over the loop's true state, closes its mass and energy budget continuously, and forecasts where it is heading with calibrated uncertainty.

07 — Remediation, in depth

Verify the cleanup, not just the plan.

Bioremediation works when microbes and plants metabolize contaminants faster than they spread. The hard part has never been deploying the biology — it is proving, continuously and defensibly, that the system is actually drawing the contaminant down rather than stalling. Terra closes the redox and mass budget of the treatment zone and reports progress with calibrated uncertainty.

  • Redox and electron-donor budgets tracked to closure
  • Contaminant drawdown estimated between lab samples, not just at them
  • Stall and rebound detected early, before a site falls out of compliance
  • Regulator-ready record with uncertainty stated, not hidden

08 — The Mission · BLSS

Everything we build points at bioregenerative life support.

A bioregenerative life-support system (BLSS) recycles a crew's air, water, and waste through living nitrogen and carbon cycles — with no margin and no resupply. It is the reason Terra exists. Unlike the physico-chemical scrubbers on today's stations, a BLSS is biological, and it fails the way every closed living system fails: at the level of the coupled biogeochemical state.

NASA has named the unsolved pieces in writing — the nutrient loop isn't closed, the microbial and plant communities aren't understood, and there is no autonomous system to detect and respond to off-nominal events. Every one is a biogeochemical state problem. Every one is what the engine does.
