01 — The Platform

A single engine that closes the budget.

Living systems are noisy, partly observed, and never at steady state. Terra holds a probability distribution over a system's true state, closes its mass and energy budget continuously, and forecasts where it is heading, with calibrated uncertainty. It runs on the edge, next to the loop, with no cloud required.

/02

04 — Remediation

Verify the cleanup, not just the plan.

Living systems that clean ground, watched in real time.

Bioremediation works when microbes and plants metabolize contaminants faster than they spread. The hard part has never been deploying the biology. It is proving, continuously and defensibly, that the system is actually drawing the contaminant down rather than stalling. Terra closes the redox and mass budget of the treatment zone and reports progress with calibrated uncertainty, turning a slow, sampling-driven guess into a live control loop.

  • Redox and electron-donor budgets tracked to closure
  • Contaminant drawdown estimated between lab samples, not just at them
  • Stall and rebound detected early, before a site falls out of compliance
  • Regulator-ready record with uncertainty stated, not hidden
05 — Life Support

The same engine,
keeping a crew alive.

A bioregenerative life-support system is a closed loop where atmosphere, plants, water, and microbes regenerate each other. There is no margin and no resupply. It is the most unforgiving version of the exact problem we solve on Earth every day. We harden the framework on a fish farm, in a field, and in a research habitat, then run it where the budget has to stay closed or people do not come home.
