Wastewater treatment plant
The same failure mode

Cleaning water is a living process.

Wastewater treatment and environmental remediation run on living biology: nitrifying and denitrifying communities, microbial consortia, delicate redox balances. Like a fish tank, the process drifts as a whole before any single sensor crosses a line, and an upset is expensive to recover from. Operators usually find out late.

How the engine transfers

Terra is one engine, and a new domain is a specification rather than a rebuild. The same state estimator that reconstructs a hidden aquaculture variable can reconstruct the health of a treatment process, forecast an upset ahead of time, and act within safety-bounded limits, on the edge and offline.

  • Forecast process upsets before effluent goes out of spec.
  • Separate true process drift from probe fouling and noise.
  • Keep a full audit log for every automated action, which matters where discharge is regulated.
Where this stands

This is on Terra’s roadmap, after the aquaculture beachhead. It is not deployed yet, and we would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise. We are interested in design partners running municipal or industrial process water who want to shape the first build.
